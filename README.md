This forecaster pins each market's median to its last traded price and builds the five quantiles around it from that market's own recent volatility: one-minute moves are blended from a short recent window and a longer one, with recent minutes weighted more, then scaled and capped, so a market that has been moving gets a wider spread than one that has been still. The spread grows with the horizon to the power 0.4, widens further after a large recent jump, and narrows when the market has barely moved, keeping every forecast inside zero and one.
